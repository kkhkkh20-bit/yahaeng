# First mission clarity — v197

A fresh quick start now teaches one action at a time inside the real first mission: walk, crouch, approach the marked target, assassinate and leave. The guide does not lock attacks, victory or alternative routes. Existing clears and older in-progress checkpoints continue without repeating these controls. An early knockout yields immediately to unfinished-target recovery; it cannot grant victory or trap the player in movement instructions. Completed control steps are saved through the normal safe-checkpoint rules.

The opening briefing keeps the mission and departure button prominent. The full skill tree remains available through the existing menu; the first result invites the player to spend their four starting points. No skill is recommended, purchased or granted automatically.

Attack hints distinguish approaching from actual melee contact. Pressing too far away explains how to recover, while the existing movement/early-press buffer stays intact. Introductory paper, uniform, camera and stair goals advertise the action key only when the selected physical action matches their object. The first assassination also distinguishes being in attack range from having a clear witness-free opportunity.

Search feedback persists after a toast disappears and distinguishes a body report, a real player sighting and CCTV. A body alone does not identify the player; real witness memory and shared appearance reports still apply. Work-risk previews use conditional language. A recent personal takedown takes precedence over an implicit lamp selection, while deliberately selected objects and useful clothing keep their normal behavior. The selected body keeps its risk outline without a duplicate risk caption.
